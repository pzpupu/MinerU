- No negative marking for wrong answer in this round.

- You have 30 sec to answer direct questions and 5 sec for pass questions.

- Each team will have 2 visuals and each visual will have 2 questions.

- Total 10 questions for this round.

- 10 points for the right answer.

- After buzzer unanswered /wrongly answered questions will get 10 negative marks.

- No pass is allowed.

- You have 10 sec to answer.
